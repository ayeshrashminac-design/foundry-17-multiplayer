Original GLB files are preserved. Godot wrappers normalize model scale/orientation, correct Benelli/VSK mesh bind transforms, select and retime existing animations, hide duplicate remote hands and unused meshes, and adjust ADS arm poses and effect markers. The unrigged Russian Soldier is scaled into two static training mannequins; it supplies no movement or death clips.
