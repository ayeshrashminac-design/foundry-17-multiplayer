# 🛸 FPS Bunker: Alien Invaders — Godot 4 Playable Experience

A AAA-quality, highly detailed 3D sci-fi first-person shooter level set in **Sector X-17: Biological Research Bunker**, invaded and overrun by hostile extraterrestrial forces.

---

## 🎮 Controls & Gameplay Mechanics

| Action | Key / Input | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Movement** | `W`, `A`, `S`, `D` | Smooth directional bunker movement |
| **Look** | `Mouse` | First-person camera with dynamic recoil & sway |
| **Shoot** | `Left Mouse Click` | Fire equipped weapon |
| **Aim Down Sights (ADS)** | `Right Mouse Click` | Precision zoom & lowered recoil |
| **Weapon 1: Assault Rifle** | `1` | High-rate kinetic bunker rifle |
| **Weapon 2: Shotgun** | `2` | 8-pellet heavy close-range scattergun |
| **Weapon 3: Plasma Carbine** | `3` | Rapid-fire alien plasma projectile weapon |
| **Weapon 4: Heavy Cannon** | `4` | High-damage explosive plasma cannon |
| **Cycle Weapons** | `Mouse Scroll Wheel` | Next / Previous weapon |
| **Reload** | `R` | Reload current magazine |
| **Jump** | `Space` | Jump over obstacles |
| **Sprint** | `Shift` | High-speed sprint with FOV boost |
| **Interact** | `E` | Read lore terminals & access data logs |
| **Pause / Release Mouse** | `Escape` | Toggle mouse capture |

## 🗺️ Level Areas & Environmental Storytelling

1. **Area 01 — Sector X-17 Bunker Entrance**:
   - Heavy blast door, automated turret emplacements, security barriers, weapon racks, breached perimeter.
2. **Area 02 — Main Military Corridors**:
   - Hexagonal bulkheads, overhead conduits, cable trays, floor LED runner tracks, steam leaks, damaged panels.
3. **Area 03 — Central Combat Arena**:
   - Multi-level 2-story combat zone with elevated catwalks, stairs, industrial machinery, and tactical cover.
4. **Area 04 — Alien Research Laboratory**:
   - Glass cryogenic pods, bio-containment cylinders with glowing specimens, holograms, DNA analyzers.
5. **Area 05 — Alien Infestation & Bio-Growth Zone**:
   - Biomechanical vines, bioluminescent veins, alien egg clusters, toxic acid pools, and destructible bio-pods.
6. **Area 06 — Reactor / Power Core Landmark**:
   - Giant rotating energy core cylinder, high-voltage coolant conduits, electrical arcs, and steam plumes.
7. **Area 07 — Security Command Center**:
   - Surveillance walls, tactical planning holographic maps, emergency lockdown terminals, lore logs.
8. **Area 08 — Subterranean Alien Chamber (Boss Arena)**:
   - Deep crystalline cavern with alien glyph pillars, alien portals, and the final Alien Commander boss battle.

## 👾 Alien Roster

- **Alien Soldier**: Fast, predatory, strafes while firing cyan plasma bolts.
- **Alien Heavy Soldier**: Armored brute with chest power core and heavy plasma cannon.
- **Alien Stalker**: Stealth ambush predator with rapid claw attacks.
- **Parasite Swarmers**: Fast swarming crawler entities hatching from alien eggs.
- **Alien Commander (Boss)**: 4-meter biomechanical boss featuring energy shields, triple-spread plasma bursts, piercing laser beam, and minion swarms.

## 🔊 Procedural Audio & VFX Engine
- **Sound**: Realistic procedural audio synthesis for all weapons, bullet impacts, alien screeches, boss roars, explosions, and UI sound effects without external audio files.
- **VFX**: Dynamic sparks, alien blood/slime splatters, volumetric fog, plasma explosions, and screen shake.
